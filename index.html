Client Vault

Client Vault is a zero-knowledge, end-to-end encrypted vault for storing client credentials (domains, hosting providers, servers) with TOTP two-step verification, device-unlock support, and optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.

  • Your Master Password never leaves your device.
  • Everything is encrypted on your device (Argon2id + AES-256-GCM) before it is stored or synced.
  • Optional sync stores only an encrypted file in the hidden app-data area of your own Google Drive.
