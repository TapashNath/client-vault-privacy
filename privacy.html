Client Vault - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Client Vault ("the app") is an encrypted vault for storing client credentials. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not do. In short: we do not collect, receive, store, or share your data. The developer operates no server for this app.

1. Data stored in the app

The credentials, notes, server details and settings you enter are encrypted on your device with a key derived from your Master Password and a device Secret Key (Argon2id, AES-256-GCM). The Master Password and keys are never sent anywhere. The developer cannot read your data and cannot recover it if you lose your Master Password, Secret Key and Recovery Key.

2. Optional cloud sync (Google Firebase)

If you choose to turn on sync, you sign in with your Google account. The app uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication only to identify you (your account ID and email address). Your already-encrypted vault file is then stored in a Firebase Realtime Database (a Google service) under your own account ID, so your other devices can receive changes in real time. Database rules allow only you, signed in with your own account, to read or write that data. Google and the developer cannot read the vault contents because they are encrypted on your device before upload; the keys never leave your devices.

The app requests only basic Google sign-in information (account ID and email). It does not access your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or any other Google data, and it does not read or write your Google Password Manager. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Client Vault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google is used only to sign you in and provide the sync feature, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

3. Device unlock (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint)

If you enable device unlock, authentication is performed by your operating system. The app never receives your biometric data; it only learns whether the system check succeeded. A vault key is kept in your device's secure keychain/keystore for this feature, and you can turn it off at any time.

4. SSH connections

If you use the built-in SSH terminal or server tools, the app connects directly from your device to the servers you configured, using the credentials you stored. No traffic passes through the developer or any third-party service.

5. No analytics, ads, or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs and makes no network requests other than those you trigger (cloud sync, SSH connections, opening links).

6. Clipboard

When you copy a password or username, it is placed on the system clipboard and automatically cleared after the time you set in Settings.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data stays on your device and, if sync is enabled, as an encrypted file in the Firebase Realtime Database under your account ID. To delete it, turn sync off and use "Reset this device" in Settings, then uninstall the app. To have the cloud copy deleted, open an issue (link below) with your account email and it will be removed.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

10. Contact

Questions can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/TapashNath/client-vault-privacy/issues.
