Client Vault - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Client Vault ("the app") is an encrypted vault for storing client credentials. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not do. In short: we do not collect, receive, store, or share your data. The developer operates no server for this app.

1. Data stored in the app

The credentials, notes, server details and settings you enter are encrypted on your device with a key derived from your Master Password and a device Secret Key (Argon2id, AES-256-GCM). The Master Password and keys are never sent anywhere. The developer cannot read your data and cannot recover it if you lose your Master Password, Secret Key and Recovery Key.

2. Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for the single scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This gives the app access only to its own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read, or change any other file in your Drive. The only file stored there is your already-encrypted vault file; Google (and the developer) cannot read its contents. Your Google account email is used only to sign in and is shown inside the app. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Client Vault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the sync feature, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

3. Device unlock (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint)

If you enable device unlock, authentication is performed by your operating system. The app never receives your biometric data; it only learns whether the system check succeeded. A vault key is kept in your device's secure keychain/keystore for this feature, and you can turn it off at any time.

4. SSH connections

If you use the built-in SSH terminal or server tools, the app connects directly from your device to the servers you configured, using the credentials you stored. No traffic passes through the developer or any third-party service.

5. No analytics, ads, or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs and makes no network requests other than those you trigger (Google Drive sync, SSH connections, opening links).

6. Clipboard

When you copy a password or username, it is placed on the system clipboard and automatically cleared after the time you set in Settings.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data stays on your device and, if enabled, in your own Google Drive app-data folder. To delete it, use "Reset this device" in Settings, disconnect Google Drive, and uninstall the app. You can remove the synced file by revoking the app's access in your Google Account.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

10. Contact

Questions can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/TapashNath/client-vault-privacy/issues.
